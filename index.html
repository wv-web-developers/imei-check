IMEI Check

Paste one IMEI per line. Dial *#06# on a phone to see its IMEI.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. This checks that a number is a well-formed IMEI; it cannot tell you whether a phone is blacklisted, stolen or carrier-locked. Make and model come from the Osmocom TAC database (CC-BY-SA 3.0), which is community-collected and thin on recent phones.
